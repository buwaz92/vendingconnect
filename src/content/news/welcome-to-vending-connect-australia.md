We've launched our new website at vendingconnect.com.au.

Here's what you'll find:

- **A free vending machine for suitable sites.** If you run a workplace, warehouse, gym or apartment building, you can [check if your location qualifies](/contact/). Busy sites may also earn commission.
- **Vending guides.** Practical advice on [starting a vending business](/guides/how-to-start-a-vending-business-in-australia/), choosing machines, finding locations and fixing common faults.
- **Updates** on the vending industry in Australia.

We'll be adding new guides regularly. Follow us on Facebook to see them first.
