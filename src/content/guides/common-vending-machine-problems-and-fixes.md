Most vending machine faults are simple, and you can fix many of them yourself in a few minutes. Here are the most common ones we see, and what to check first.

**Safety first:** turn off and unplug the machine before you touch anything inside, except for simple checks on the front panel. Leave electrical and refrigeration work to a qualified technician.

## Product gets stuck and doesn't drop

This is the number one complaint.

**What to check:**

- Is the product loaded the right way in the spiral? Bottles and bags need to sit straight.
- Is the spiral the right size for that product? Small items in a big spiral can twist and catch.
- Is the spiral bent or out of position?
- Is the drop sensor at the bottom dirty or blocked?

**Fix:** reload the spiral properly, swap to the correct spiral size, and wipe the sensor with a dry cloth.

## Coin jam or coins being rejected

**What to check:**

- Open the coin mech and look for jammed coins, foreign coins or rubbish.
- Check that the coin tubes aren't overfilled.
- Clean the coin path with a soft brush.

**Fix:** clear the jam, clean the path, and empty the tubes if they're too full. If it keeps rejecting good coins, the coin mech may need recalibrating or replacing.

## Note reader not taking notes

**What to check:**

- Is the stacker full?
- Are the sensors dirty? Many readers have a cleaning card you can run through.
- Is the note crinkled or damaged?

**Fix:** empty the stacker and clean the sensors.

## Card reader offline

**What to check:**

- Does the reader have power and a signal? Look at the status lights.
- Has the SIM or network dropped out? Many readers use a mobile signal, and some spots in a building get poor reception.
- Has the reader's account or plan lapsed?

**Fix:** restart the reader (or the whole machine), check the signal, and contact your card reader provider if it stays offline. Sometimes moving the antenna helps.

## Fridge not cooling properly

This one costs the most if it's ignored, especially in summer.

**What to check:**

- Is there a gap behind the machine for airflow? It needs room to breathe.
- Are the condenser coils full of dust?
- Is the machine in direct sun or next to a heat source?
- Is the door seal damaged?

**Fix:** clean the coils, move the machine out from the wall, and check the seal. If it still won't cool, call a refrigeration technician.

## Display or lights not working

**What to check:**

- Is the machine plugged in and switched on at the wall?
- Has a safety switch or fuse tripped?
- Are the LED strips loose?

**Fix:** check the power first. If the machine runs but the display is dead, the display board or its cable may need replacing.

## Machine says "sold out" but there's stock

**What to check:**

- Are the selection and price settings right after the last restock?
- Is the motor for that row working?

**Fix:** go back into the service menu and check the row settings, then test the motor.

## Prevent problems before they start

- Clean the coils every few months
- Wipe the sensors and coin path on every restock
- Load products carefully, with the right spiral for each
- Check the card reader is online after each visit
- Keep a log of faults so you spot patterns
